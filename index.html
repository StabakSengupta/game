The Last Seed — Prototype

Guide the Last Seed across a ruined land to find fertile soil. Use arrow keys or touch controls.

Prototype · single-file · suitable for GitHub Pages

Story (short)

After a long apocalypse the world is barren. You're the Last Seed — a tiny life-bearing kernel on a desperate journey to reach the last fertile patch of soil. Along the way you'll avoid hazards, gather nutrients, and awaken dormant life.

How to play

  1. Arrow keys or WASD to move the seed.
  2. Collect green nutrient orbs to grow energy.
  3. Avoid rock obstacles and toxic clouds — they damage your seed.
  4. Reach the big green patch (Target Soil) to win.

Deploy to GitHub Pages
